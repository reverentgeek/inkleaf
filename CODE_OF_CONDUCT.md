# Code of Conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] as its code of conduct. Please give it a read, it's short.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The short version: everyone taking part in this project, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else, is expected to be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't welcome here.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that crosses the line, email me privately at **david@reverentgeek.com**. Reports are handled discreetly, and nobody gets retaliated against for reporting in good faith.

As the only maintainer, enforcement is on me. I may edit, hide, or delete contributions, or block accounts, in response to behavior I consider inappropriate. If your report is *about* me, please take it to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/) instead.
